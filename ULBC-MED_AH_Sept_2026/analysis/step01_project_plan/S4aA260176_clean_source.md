## 1. Introduction

<a id="P009"></a>

At TSG SA#112 plenary meeting held in Singapore, the ULBC-MED WID in SP-260696 was approved [1], in particular, it was reiterated the importance to complete the WI within Rel-20 timeframe. Due to this time pressure, a series of informal offline calls was organized by the Rapporteur with good participations despite the summer vacation period. The goal of having those offline calls was to collect early input from delegates on some inevitable discussion topics that will take place in formal SA4 and/or Audio SWG meetings, and try to converge onto some potentially agreeable positions for SA4 to decide at #137-e meeting.

<a id="P011"></a>

This contribution proposes a draft version of Permanent document ULBC-2, Timeline for ULBC-MED WI.

## 1.    Discussion

<a id="P008"></a>

Given the envisioned application scenarios of ULBC codec in Clause 4, TR 26.940 targeting IMS voice service over GEO and NGSO, the codec needs to meet a quality bar that the end users have come to expect based on the deployed VoLTE IMS voice services. The source recommends the following performance requirements to be considered for ULBC.

<a id="P010"></a>

Based on TR 26.940 Clause 8, the recommended performance requirements for ULBC codec at 1kbps is AMR-WB 12.65kbps. Since the second bitrate that is considered is around 1.4kbps, and not 3kbps, the proposal is to consider EVS-WB 13.2kbps.

<a id="P012"></a>

## 1.1    Performance Requirements

<a id="P013"></a>

The ULBC codec shall meet the following performance requirements in clean speech and noisy speech testing.

<a id="P015"></a>

the 1.0 kbps operating point must be Not Worse Than (NWT) AMR-WB 12.65 kbps, and

<a id="P016"></a>

the 1.4 kbps operating point must be Not Worse Than (NWT) EVS-WB 13.2 kbps,

<a id="P017"></a>

The following reference codecs and operating points shall be considered for clean speech and noisy speech testing (where appropriate for transcoding and non-transcoding testing).

<a id="P019"></a>

-    AMR: 12.2kbps

<a id="P020"></a>

-    AMR-WB: 8.85kbps and 12.65kbps

<a id="P021"></a>

-    EVS-WB: 7.2kbps and 13.2kbps

<a id="P023"></a>

## 2.    Proposal

<a id="P024"></a>

It is proposed to agree to the above performance requirements and include in ULBC-3 Permanent document.

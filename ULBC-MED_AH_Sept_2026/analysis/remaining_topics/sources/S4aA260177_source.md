Introduction

<a id="P011"></a>

In ULBC-3 v0.1.0 (ULBC Performance Requirements) [1], Section 5 defines in Clause 5 the ULBC Objective Performance Requirements; however, the methodology specified in ULBC-7 [2] does not reference the audio material to be used. This input document proposes a test set for objective requirements, based on the database defined in EVS permanent document EVS-7a, clause 5.4.1 [3].

Table 1 – datasets description for objective evaluation from [3]

<a id="P021"></a>

The database comprises clean speech, speech mixed with background noises, as well as mixed-content and music material in narrowband (NB), wideband (WB), and super-wideband (SWB) formats.

<a id="P023"></a>

The sources of these databases are specified in EVS-8a, Clause H.2.1 [4]. The clean speech database is a multilingual corpus assembled from established speech-quality test datasets, including ITU-T P.501 and P.501 Amd.1 [5], the speech portion of the ETSI STQ database [6], and additional contributions provided under a multi-party NDA. The corpus was designed to contain approximately 40% active speech and includes a 10-second preamble.

<a id="P025"></a>

The noisy speech databases were generated by mixing the clean speech corpus with background noise recordings contributed under the same multi-party NDA framework.

<a id="P027"></a>

The EVS datasets are provided in NB (8 kHz), WB (16 kHz), and SWB (32 kHz) formats as raw headerless PCM audio files.

<a id="P029"></a>

The EVS approach provides a useful reference for ULBC-MED, as it defines a common and reproducible test corpus for objective performance evaluation. However, several aspects of the EVS corpus are not directly aligned with the scope of the current ULBC-MED work item:

<a id="P030"></a>

ULBC-MED targets WB and SWB operation; NB content is therefore not required.

<a id="P031"></a>

The performance requirements defined in ULBC-3 focus on speech coding performance and do not require objective evaluation for mixed-content and music material.

<a id="P032"></a>

Part of the original EVS corpus relies on material distributed under a multi-party NDA, which may complicate the accessibility.

<a id="P033"></a>

Proposal

<a id="P034"></a>

It is proposed that a common corpus be defined for evaluating the objective performance requirements specified in ULBC-3. The corpus is proposed to consist exclusively of:

<a id="P035"></a>

Clean speech material;

<a id="P036"></a>

Noisy speech material generated by mixing speech with representative background noises;

<a id="P037"></a>

WB (16 kHz) and SWB (32 kHz) sampling rates.

<a id="P039"></a>

NB speech, mixed-content and music material are proposed to be excluded, as they are not relevant to the current scope of the ULBC-MED standardization effort.

<a id="P041"></a>

To maximize accessibility, the source further proposes that the corpus be assembled exclusively from publicly available material. ITU-T P.501 appears to be a natural foundation for such a corpus, as it is widely used for speech-quality testing and is available under licensing conditions suitable for standardization activities while forbidding use for training purposes.

<a id="P043"></a>

The amount of publicly available speech material in ITU-T P.501 alone may not be sufficient to generate long test sequences (around up to 5 minutes). Additional speech material could therefore be drawn from publicly available ETSI STQ datasets [6].

<a id="P045"></a>

For noisy speech evaluation, the background-noise material provided by ITU-T P.501 and ETSI STQ is relatively limited in duration. As an alternative, environmental recordings from the DEMAND dataset [7] could be used to generate noisy speech test items. While the individual DEMAND recordings are typically limited to approximately five minutes, they represent a broader range of acoustic environments than the three noise conditions used in the EVS corpus. Consequently, a corpus duration of up to five minutes per test item is proposed, while increasing the diversity of background noise conditions compared to EVS processing plan.

<a id="P047"></a>

It is proposed to agree on:

<a id="P049"></a>

A dataset for objective evaluation is to be defined, consisting of:

<a id="P050"></a>

Clean speech material;

<a id="P051"></a>

Noisy speech material generated by mixing speech with representative background noises;

<a id="P053"></a>

As a baseline ITU-T P.501 (04/25) Annex C is used for the speech. Noise material is TBD.

<a id="P055"></a>

Editor’s note: Noise material might be proposed by 3GPP members, as long as it is donated for the ULBC work and under constraint that it is not allowed to use for the training of candidate codecs.

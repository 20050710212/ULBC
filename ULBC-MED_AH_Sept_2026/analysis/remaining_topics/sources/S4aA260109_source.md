<a id="P050"></a>

According to [23228], there exist two Layer-3 transport mechanisms for IMS voice packets over NB-IoT NTN:

<a id="P051"></a>

IP transport mechanism: Transport mechanism of IP packets shall be supported, use of Robust Header Compression (RoHC) is recommended for this case.

<a id="P052"></a>

Non-IP transport mechanism or UHRR: Transport mechanism of voice packets using removal and restoration of parts of RTP/UDP/IP headers in the UE and network may be supported by the UE and the network (i.e., it is optional in the UE and in the network)

<a id="P053"></a>

Packet Gateway (PGW) removes and restores UDP/IP headers

<a id="P054"></a>

UE removes/compresses and restores/decompresses the RTP header, and removes and restores UDP/IP headers.

<a id="P055"></a>

Figure 5.2.1-2 illustrates where the above actions occur in the system for the non-IP layer-3 transport mechanism for IMS voice over NB-IoT NTN.

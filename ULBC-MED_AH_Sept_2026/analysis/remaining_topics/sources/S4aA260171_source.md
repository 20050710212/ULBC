Introduction

<a id="P007"></a>

The survey initiated by the SA4 Chair prior to the SA4#137-e meeting concluded that there is “A clear majority, with no objection raised” on requiring that test material is not used as training data (see S4-261674). In the present Tdoc we discuss the implications for noise material collection.

For ULBC-MED the AMR-WB approach seems to be reasonable. This can ensure that the test dataset will be distinct from the training dataset (for the noise part).

<a id="P015"></a>

Proposal

<a id="P016"></a>

It is proposed that the Test Labs will be in charge of providing clean speech files and a Noise Laboratory that will be in charge of providing (pre-processed) noise files required for ULBC-MED selection testing. It is expected that these entities will guarantee that neither clean speech nor noise files have been seen in training.

<a id="P017"></a>

SA4 should invite interested parties to declare which languages and noise types they can support and provide a quotation to estimate the necessary budget for recording clean speech and noise files and/or making such samples available.

<a id="P018"></a>

NOTE: the Noise Laboratory entity may be split among different “providers” (e.g. one “provider” per noise type). A Test Lab may also be a “provider”.
